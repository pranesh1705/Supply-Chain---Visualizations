Before you start

You will be randomly assigned one of four supply-chain roles based on your roll number. You will play that role for a series of rounds; the other three tiers are run automatically. Your objective is to manage your inventory as well as you can — you'll see exactly how that's measured on the next screen.

Your objective

Every round costs you money in two ways: holding cost on any stock sitting unused in your warehouse, and backlog cost on any order you couldn't fulfil on time (backlog cost is charged at twice the rate of holding cost, since a stockout is more costly than a bit of extra stock). Your goal is to place orders that keep your total cost as low as possible across all rounds — not simply to avoid ever running short, and not simply to hoard stock either. You are optimizing your own tier's cost, not the whole chain's — exactly like a real manager would.

How this works

  • Every round, you will see the order placed on you by the tier below (or true customer demand, if you are the Retailer), your current stock on hand, any backlog, and stock already on the way to you — shown both as numbers and as charts.
  • You decide how much to order from the tier above you. There is no hidden "correct" answer — order what you think keeps cost lowest.
  • Orders take time to arrive (the lead time) — just like a real supply chain.
  • Some real-world complications may apply this round set — watch for notes about minimum order quantities or delayed promotion information as you play.
  • You will play 20 rounds, then your instructor will share a password to reveal your results and continue.
