The story

You run a sandwich stall. Every morning you decide how many sandwiches Q to order from the bakery, before you know how many customers will come. Today's demand is the sum of two dice (2 to 12, most likely 7). You sell each sandwich at ₹10. The bakery makes a sandwich for ₹1. Sandwiches left unsold at night are worthless.
